## ESI heater characterization

[`esi_heater_characterization.ipynb`](esi_heater_characterization.ipynb) runs the supervised **50 W / 100 °C** heater test and is **armed by default**. Keep `notebooks/` beside `esi/`, or set `PLUGIN_DIR` to the ESI plugin folder. Use a fresh 64-bit Windows kernel with Explorer and CGC utilities closed. Logs and unfinished-run markers remain in `esi/logs/esi_heater_characterization/`; do not move or delete them. See the notebook for the protocol, Stop and operator-restart requirements.

## Pressure versus capillary temperature

[`pressure_temperature.ipynb`](pressure_temperature.ipynb) uses ESI (COM16, 230400 baud) and TPG366 (COM21, configurable baud; default 9600). Keep `notebooks/` beside `esi/` and `tpg366/`, or set `PLUGINS_DIR` once. Windows, Python 64-bit, `pyserial`, `matplotlib` and a fresh Jupyter kernel are required. Before construction/connection the notebook checks that the loaded ESI controller provides the heater safety methods; cached ESI namespaces are refused. The SHA-256 of the runtime files used is recorded in the run metadata (traceability, not a version lock).

The single executable cell is disarmed by default. Review the COM ports and TPG USB baud, keep the PC awake with sleep/hibernation disabled, and supervise the run before setting `ARM_HEATING=True`. Close Explorer, CGC utilities and former instrument-owning kernels. TPG366 only measures pressure; 9600 baud is its software default, not a verified hardware setting.

The approved protocol starts with a 60 s heater-OFF/HV-OFF baseline, then **30, 40, …, 170, 175 °C**. A fresh initial temperature above 30 °C refuses heating rather than skipping stages. Fixed electrical ceilings are **22 V / 10 A / 50 W**, bounded to freshly read controller maxima and conservatively prepared for native rounding. Requested ceilings, prepared inputs, applied echoes and independent readbacks are recorded separately. Integer temperature targets must echo exactly; the ±0.2 °C measurement band is not a target-write tolerance.

Each stage requires a full 60 s within ±0.2 °C and absolute OLS slope <0.1 °C/min, followed by 300 s continuously qualified observation. First qualification must finish within 600 s of the command; the absolute stage budget is 900 s. Gaps over 2 s or loss of qualification reset observation, not its deadline. The 175 °C hold is attempted within 174.8–175.0 °C; the first reading >175 °C aborts it. After verified heater OFF, observe T/P for 900 s before final ESI shutdown/discharge. This does not prove pressure equilibrium, a cold assembly or material suitability at 175 °C. Sampled software checks are not an independent hardware temperature limiter. Limits remain configured; no NVM save or automatic power increase is issued.

CSV is appended and flushed after every sample; JSON and two PNG figures preserve complete or partial runs under `pressure_temperature_runs/`. Pressure is in mbar, with all six gauge statuses and separate pressure/temperature observation timestamps. Any finite pressure with status 0 permits acquisition to continue; every other gauge remains NaN with its exact status, including sensor/identification errors. No valid pressure, or a dialogue/frame/transport error, refuses or stops heating. No gauge is implicitly critical; this is not a vacuum interlock. Negative or zero valid pressures remain in CSV, not on logarithmic plots. Temperature is measured, never substituted with the setpoint or carried forward during shutdown.

Stop or a measurement error requests heater OFF, then verified ESI OFF/discharge and port closure. Each instrument has one I/O owner: Stop cancels heater-readiness waits and pending positive commands, then lets the active ESI call return before OFF, without interrupting its native join. The cancellation token does not block OFF/discharge cleanup. Pressure acquisition during shutdown is best effort and cannot block OFF; cancellation precedes serial closure by its owner, including a delayed OS opening. Unknown/poisoned ESI transport state prohibits further DLL calls. Unconfirmed shutdown or unfinished I/O retains the common per-user COM guard and historical heater/PT markers. The characterization and pressure notebooks share the same ownership/recovery helper; changing notebooks or output folders does not resolve recorded uncertainty. An explicit run-specific operator declaration is required for recovery after physical safety is established and former owners are terminated. Original evidence and True/False shutdown values are archived unchanged; the declaration is not confirmation of HV discharge. The report distinguishes ESI shutdown from pressure-link closure. Restarting the kernel is not an OFF. No automatic reconnect, configuration load or interlock bypass. TPG366's intermittent NAKs still require hardware diagnosis; this notebook does not conceal or retry them.

Close ESIBD Explorer and CGC utilities; check COM ports and load/material compatibility before running either notebook.
